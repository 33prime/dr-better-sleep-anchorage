Frequently Asked Questions

How does the mouthpiece actually work?

It's a mandibular advancement device: it holds your lower jaw slightly forward (up to 8 mm across five strap positions), keeping the soft tissue at the back of the throat from collapsing into the airway — the collapse that makes the snoring sound.

Will it fit my mouth?

Yes — that's the point of boil-and-bite. Soften it in hot water, bite for thirty seconds, and it holds a custom impression of your teeth. Misfit? Re-boil and redo it, guided step by step in the app.

Is the app included?

Every device ships with app access included — tracking, snore-type analysis, audio clips, and the Dr. Sommers coach. No subscription for preorder customers.

Can the app tell me if I have sleep apnea?

No — and be wary of any phone app that claims it can. A microphone measures snoring; diagnosing apnea requires a clinical study. If your data shows patterns worth a professional's eyes, the app will say so, plainly.
