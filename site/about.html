About Dr. Better Sleep

Clinically inspired sleep solutions born from nearly 30 years of dental expertise and a passion for improving lives through better sleep.

Meet our founder

Dr. Howard Sommers

DDS, D-ABDSM — Diplomate, American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine

Dr. Howard Sommers is the visionary behind Dr. Better Sleep, a company dedicated to developing innovative sleep aid products. With nearly 30 years in dentistry, his path is an unusual one — it began as an ironworker helping to construct the World Trade Center before he pursued a career defined by dedication and an unwavering commitment to patient care.

He earned his DDS from New York University's College of Dentistry, graduating Phi Beta Kappa with a Bachelor of Science in Biology, and deepened his training through advanced programs at Albert Einstein Medical Center and Mid Continent Orthodontics, with IV Sedation certification from Oregon Health & Science University.

His Diplomate status with the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine positions him at the front line of treating sleep-disordered breathing through dental approaches — working closely with physicians to offer alternatives when patients can't tolerate CPAP. Beyond dental sleep medicine, he is certified by the American Academy of Facial Esthetics in treating TMJ disorders and migraines.

  • D-ABDSM — Board Certified
  • IV Sedation Certified — OHSU

  • TMJ & Migraine — AAFE Certified
